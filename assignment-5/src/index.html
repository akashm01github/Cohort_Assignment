The Victoria Memorial is a famous historical monument in Kolkata. Built in memory of Queen Victoria, it is made of white marble and surrounded by beautiful gardens. The museum inside displays paintings, artifacts, and history, making it one of Kolkata’s top tourist attractions. It also reflects British architecture and attracts thousands of visitors every year from different parts of India and abroad.
